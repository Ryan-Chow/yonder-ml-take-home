## Train/val leakage

The task says images are augmented variants of shared source photos (`<orig>_jpg.rf.<hash>.jpg`). In this sample that is **not** how leakage shows up: `sampling/sample-roboflow.py` keeps one `.rf.` copy per source photo, so the exact-stem check finds nothing. Same-origin images still cross the split through other paths, so each val image is checked against train with four rules:

- **exact stem**: same `<orig>` before `.rf.`.
- **family**: frames of one video (`<video>_mp4-<n>`) and `-copy` duplicates of one photo.
- **sequence**: consecutive capture numbers in one series (`IMG_0619`/`IMG_0620`, `t_bot_mal262`/`263`, gap <= 2) or phone timestamps within 30 s (`20231021_173204`). Manual precision check (30 random cross-split sequence pairs, seed 42, contact sheet reviewed by eye): 28 same scene and object, 2 same bottle and session with a different background, 0 unrelated.
- **phash**: perceptual hash of the central 40% of the image, Hamming distance <= 10. The centre crop is deliberate: the black corners from Roboflow's rotation made whole-image hashes match unrelated rotated photos at distance 8. 13 near-uniform crops (white synthetic collages) are excluded, because they all hash identically.

| rule | val images with a same-origin train image | % of 199 val |
|---|---|---|
| exact stem | 0 | 0% |
| family | 8 | 4% |
| sequence | 93 | 47% |
| phash | 0 | 0% |
| any (connected groups) | 107 | 54% |

Connected components over all rules: **532 groups** for 999 images (largest 46, singletons 365). **107 of 199 val images (54%) share a group with at least one train image.** Metrics on the original split therefore partly measure recall of near-duplicates. `scripts/make_split.py` re-splits by group.
